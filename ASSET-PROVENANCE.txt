Scope: Development/Assets, application ICO files, and embedded UI resources.
Original Gurux assets retain their upstream attribution and project GPLv2 terms. New migration assets are distributed under GPL-2.0-only to the extent copyright applies. This is a licensing declaration for contributions, not an assertion of exclusive ownership or trademark permission.

The migration's ModernIcons, SoftDuoIcons, StudioOutlineIcons and StudioTreeIcons families have editable C# geometry in ModernIcons.cs, SoftIcons.cs, StudioIcons.cs and TreeIcons.cs, respectively, plus SVG exports. These code comments and prior migration records describe original geometry. No third-party icon-pack attribution was found in these files. This observation is not independent verification of originality.

FoldApplicationIcon and GMeterApplicationIcon contain editable SVGs and rendered PNG/ICO variants. Their supplied SVGs are the editable assets retained in this fork. Preserve upstream trademark rights and the unofficial-fork designation. No Gurux endorsement is claimed.

Historical export harnesses mentioned in asset READMEs were in the migration workspace and are not part of this repository. The C# geometry and ExportAssets methods are present. Do not claim those absent harness commands work in a fresh clone.

Outstanding verification: a maintainer must confirm that all contributed assets were created for this project or otherwise have permission compatible with GPLv2 before a binary release. If an asset's origin cannot be confirmed, replace it with demonstrably original artwork or a suitably licensed asset and retain its notice. This file does not invent provenance for unverified material.
